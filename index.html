Welcome to Sauna Research Studies

Sauna Research Studies is your comprehensive resource for evidence-based information on the health benefits and physiological effects of regular sauna bathing. We compile and summarize peer-reviewed research from leading medical journals, making the science accessible to everyone — from healthcare professionals and researchers to sauna enthusiasts and wellness seekers.

Sauna bathing has been an integral part of Finnish culture for thousands of years, but only in recent decades has the scientific community begun to systematically investigate its health effects. The results are remarkable: regular sauna use is associated with reduced risk of cardiovascular disease, lower blood pressure, improved cognitive function, better stress management, enhanced athletic recovery, and increased longevity.

The traditional Finnish sauna — a cornerstone of wellness for over two millennia.
